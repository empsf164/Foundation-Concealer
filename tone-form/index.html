Undertone Science

Decoding Your Sub-Surface Tone

Use these visual cues as a beauty-matching guide. Real skin reflects rich undertone complexities that define how makeup looks in direct light.

Analyze My Undertone
COOL SPECTRUM

Cool Undertones

Characterized by warm pink, rose, ruby, or subtle blue undertones. Veins appear predominantly blue/purple; silver jewelry naturally flatters the skin.

Best Codes: C110, C210, C310, C510
NEUTRAL SPECTRUM

Neutral Undertones

An even balance of golden and rosy pigments. Veins appear blue-green; both gold and silver jewelry look complementary and natural on the complexion.

Best Codes: N120, N220, N320, N620
WARM SPECTRUM

Warm Undertones

Characterized by golden, honey, yellow, peach, or warm olive notes. Veins appear olive/green; gold jewelry radiates effortlessly against the skin.

Best Codes: W130, W230, W330, W530

Visual guidance note: Undertone suggestions are approximate beauty-matching recommendations for cosmetic selection, not medical or dermatological diagnostics.
