Precision Complexion Engineering

Traditional heavy foundations suffocate skin texture under opaque chalk pigments. TONE / FORM utilizes micro-encapsulated liquid pigments suspended in a breathable hydro-matrix that adheres flexibly to facial movement.

  • Non-comedogenic, formulated without clogging waxes
  • Free from synthetic fragrance and parabens
  • Neutralizes oxidation so shade stays true from morning to evening
FORMULA HIGHLIGHTS

Ingredients selected for moisture harmony:

Sugarcane Squalane: Locks in all-day elasticity.
Niacinamide (2%): Evens skin tone appearance over wear.
Micro-Mica Silicas: Diffuse ambient studio light.
