Color Theory for Complexion

The Undertone Analyzer

Surface skin depth can fluctuate with seasons, but your sub-surface undertone remains constant. Use these optical cues to reveal your harmonic shade spectrum.

Optical Reference Guide

4 Sub-Surface Spectrums

Cool (C)

Skin shows soft pink, rose, or ruby blush. Looks vivid next to crisp stark white fabrics and icy platinum metals.

Neutral (N)

Equal distribution of warm yellow and cool pink pigments. Complexion holds balanced neutrality across all lighting types.

Warm (W)

Rich golden, peachy, honey, or amber glow. Harmonizes with warm ivory, earth tones, and yellow gold accents.

Olive (O)

Unique greenish or subtle muted bronze overtone. Often miscategorized as warm, but standard warm foundations look too orange or peach.

Beauty Guidance Note: Undertone suggestions are approximate beauty-matching guidance and may vary under different natural and ambient lighting conditions.
